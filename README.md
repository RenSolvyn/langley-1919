# Langley Field, 1919

A walk through the Army aeronautical laboratory at Langley Field, Virginia, as it stood in 1919. Plays in any modern browser, including phones.

**Play:** https://rensolvyn.github.io/langley-1919/

## Three ways to play

**Explore.** Walk the laboratory by day or by night and find the eight field notes.

**Survive the night.** You came back after dark for a notebook, and the doors locked behind you. Eight pages of a field journal are somewhere inside. Find them all and the front doors open. You are not alone in the building.

**Firefight.** A free for all inside the laboratory against up to three soldiers, each with their own skill. Pick a 1919 weapon, change its parts in the armory (magazines, muzzles, sights, stocks, ammunition), choose a body camera or a standard view, and deploy a small drone you can also build out (frame, battery, charge, video) once per match. Walls, doors and furniture break the way their materials would.

## Controls

| Desktop | Phone |
| --- | --- |
| WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to run | Left thumb to walk, push to the rim to run |
| Mouse to look | Right thumb to look |
| F flashlight | Light button |
| T to check your watch | It shows on its own at each chime |
| Q to ready the rocket tube once you find it, click to fire | Tube button readies it, the round button fires |
| Firefight: click to fire, right-click to aim, R reload, 1 and 2 switch weapons, C crouch, Shift while aiming holds your breath, Tab scores | Drag to look. Fire with the round button (drag it to aim while firing), a second finger while you look, or a double tap; hold to keep firing. AIM, R, C and 1\|2 on the right edge; gyro aim is in setup |
| Firefight drone: G to fly it, WASD to move, Space and C to climb and descend, mouse to turn and tilt the camera, click to drop the charge, G to return | DRN button, left thumb moves, UP and DN, double tap to drop |
| Esc or P to pause | Button at the top right |

Settings and sound are in the pause menu. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, for full screen.
